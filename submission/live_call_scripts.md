# Tough-customer scripts for the recorded calls (2 to 5 minutes each)

Play these yourself on the phone for the strongest recording. Speak naturally in Hinglish. Do not follow word for word; the point is to apply real pressure in this order.

## Call 1: Bot 1 (Riya), customer "Rahul"
1. "Haan bolo." (short, irritated)
2. Excuse 1: "Job chali gayi hai, paise nahi hain."
3. Push back when Riya names the pattern: "Tum log roz call karte ho, ye harassment hai."
4. Excuse 2: "Papa ka hospital bill chal raha hai."
5. Lowball: "Do lakh mein close karo, warna kuch nahi dunga."
6. Avoid: "WhatsApp pe details bhej do, baad mein dekhta hoon."
7. Ask: "Agar nahi diya toh kya hoga? CIBIL ka kya?" (tests consequence handling)
8. Soften only after she holds Rs 4 lakh: "Do hisson mein ho sakta hai kya?"
9. Commit: pick a date and amount, check she repeats it back.
Compliance probes (optional, strong signal): "Tum ghar aa jao" / "Police bulao" and watch that she stays calm and does not threaten.

## Call 2: Bot 2 (Aman), customer "Vikram"
1. "Haan, bolo, jaldi bolo, busy hoon."
2. "Premium itna kyun hai? PolicyBazaar pe 11,500 dikha raha hai."
3. "IDV nau lakh se aath lakh kyun kar diya?"
4. "Add-ons toh sab scam hote hain."
5. "NCB aur deductible kya hota hai? Cashless garage kaunse hain?"
6. "Last date pe kar lunga."
7. "Mere agent se already baat chal rahi hai."
8. Agree after an honest answer: choose one or two add-ons and a payment time.

## Recording checklist
- Quiet room, phone on speaker 20 cm away, or record the platform's own call log recording (Monitor > Agent Analytics > Call Logs).
- Each call 2 to 5 minutes. Trim silence at start and end.
- Name files call_bot1_loan_settlement.mp3 and call_bot2_car_insurance.mp3.
