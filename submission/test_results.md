# Test evidence (Sarvam automated tests, 21 Oct 2026)

Each suite is AI-generated by Sarvam from the agent's prompt (a simulated customer plus behaviour checks). The suites were regenerated for the second account, so counts differ between rounds.

| Agent | Round | Prompt | Passed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Riya (loan settlement) | 1 | first draft | 10 of 25 |
| Riya | 2 | hard stops, one-step negotiation | 18 of 21 |
| Riya | 3 | same, run again | 17 of 21 |
| Aman (car insurance) | 1 | first draft | 11 of 21 |
| Aman | 2 | tools removed, add-on pitch rule | 12 of 18 |
| Aman | 3 | hard brevity stops, indicative add-on prices, voicemail rule | 14 of 18 |

Run to run variation is real: the simulated customer is an LLM, so the same prompt can score a point or two differently.

What the later failures taught (and what the final prompts change):
- Riya: accepted a vague "call next week" (now: never accept it, get a date, or a specific callback time today or tomorrow); long opening turn (now: do not repeat the recorded-call line); read back a wrong date (now: repeat the customer's exact date, never invent one); one test expects collection to continue after the borrower says their mother is in the ICU. That failure is intentional: the prompt ends the pitch on hospitalisation, bereavement or self-harm, which is the compliant behaviour.
- Aman: no total premium when asked (now: indicative add-on prices, always state the running total); replies over 25 words (now: hard brevity stop); did not confirm a do-not-call request (now: explicit acknowledgement); stalled with "I will check with the team" (now: banned); Return to Invoice not offered against the IDV drop (now: offered).

The last prompt revision (v4 on both agents) could not be re-run: the free Sarvam credit on the second account ran out right after it was committed, so its scores are not claimed.
Local harness (`sim/`) is built and verified for chat and text-to-speech.
